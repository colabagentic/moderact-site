What I don't sell.

Schema markup. In a matched test of 1,885 pages that added it, AI Mode and ChatGPT citations showed no significant lift, and Google AI Overviews citations dropped significantly (Ahrefs, 2026).

llms.txt files. In a log study of 137,210 domains, 97% of those files got zero requests (Ahrefs, 2026).

Rankings. I report how often you are named across many runs, never a position taken from one answer.

Promises about outcomes. I report counts; the fix list is my best judgment, and the next receipt is the check.
